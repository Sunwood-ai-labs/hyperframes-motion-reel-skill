# Licensing

## This Repository

The skill is released under the [MIT License](https://github.com/Sunwood-ai-labs/hyperframes-motion-reel-skill/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Generated Projects

Projects produced by the skill are built only from:

- **HyperFrames** (`hyperframes` on npm) — Apache-2.0, free for commercial use at any company size
- **GSAP 3** — free, including commercial use
- Plain HTML, CSS, SVG and Canvas
- Node.js built-ins for audio generation

The skill does not introduce libraries that require a paid license.

## Why Not Remotion

Remotion is free for individuals, non-profits and for-profit companies with up to three employees, but for-profit companies with four or more employees need a paid Company License. This skill is the HyperFrames counterpart of `remotion-motion-reel`, keeping the same creative bar without that cost.
